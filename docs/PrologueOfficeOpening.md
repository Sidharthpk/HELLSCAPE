# Prologue office opening

The Prologue scene uses the furnished `Assets/New Office/Hellscape_Office_v3_Unity.fbx` for the Friday opening. Its original clean furniture materials are copied into `Assets/Generated/PrologueOffice`. Walls and floors use the previous office's clean `wall1.png` (plaster over wood panelling) and `floor1.png` (carpet), with generated mesh UVs to keep the panelling at floor level and carpet tiling at 2.5 metres. No burnt office variants are used. The previous office and its loose props are disabled in this scene.

The opening starts at the server/electrical room's operator chair. The first conversation is Omar in the maintenance/cleaner closet, lifting his bucket and then idling. Ben hits his frozen desktop and switches to his look-around idle. Nadia starts seated, stands, and idles while talking. Priya walks through the background toward reception during Nadia's conversation, plays walk-to-sit, then remains in her seated idle for the next conversation. Mercer waits beside the depot van for the delivery banter, then the van leaves and the existing morning delivery gameplay resumes.

`OfficeOpeningSequence` stores scene references for all actors, shots, and chair anchors on the Prologue director. The new Avatar FBXs use Generic rigs; each character plays clips from their own skeleton. Generated animation copies keep the original exports untouched and remove horizontal root travel so the authored Priya path controls her position. Nadia's initial seated pose is the first frame of her sit-to-stand clip. Omar's provided animation is a carry-water-bucket walk; the bucket lift is staged with that clip, followed by his supplied idle. Mercer uses his new model with a standing idle retargeted through Unity Humanoid avatars on generated copies of the Mercer and idle FBXs. This accommodates their different bind poses without changing the originals. The server control desk's baked textures are connected as surface colour, with their accidental full-white emission removed.

`HellScape > Prologue > New office opening` rebuilds this setup explicitly. It backs up the original scene to `Backups/PrologueOffice-20261023` on its first run. It does not change OfficeSilo or MainGameScene.

Editor automation uses `Library/PrologueOfficePass.request`: `refresh` imports edited assets, `apply` rebuilds and saves the scene, and `review` runs the actual opening with automatic dialogue advance. Review screenshots and results live in `Library/PrologueOfficePreview` and `Library/PrologueOfficeReview.result`. Review settings do not persist to normal gameplay.
