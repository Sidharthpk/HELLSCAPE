# HELLSCAPE — Story Bible & Script, Version 4.0 (final candidate)

Replaces V3 (`hellscape-story-v3.md`). It builds on your V2 GDD and V3 and applies every fix from the 17 Oct review. Unity and `HellscapeDialogue.csv` are untouched. Approve this text first, then migrate it.

**One-line pitch.** A night-shift delivery driver is murdered the morning his brother "dies". In a blood-red copy of his city he learns his van carried the dead, and that a judge can't judge the dead until someone learns their names.

**Theme.** Names vs. numbers. Every crime in this story erases a name: bodies sold as "unclaimed", a cleaner filed as an accident, a brother buried under a stranger's name, a driver turned into a wanted man nobody will ever find. The hero's job is to give names back. That's also the sequel.

### 1.2 What really happened
- **13 April, 03:10. Silo Two.** Tomas finds bodies in a company van. Vane is there. He threatens Derek ("your brother's name is on every manifest") and believes he forced him. Derek stabs Tomas and plays reluctant for Vane and the CCTV. Afterwards he phones Kessler. Vane deletes the footage. The office files it as an accident.
- **13–18 April.** Tomas's remains sit in Silo Two cold storage. Harlow Clinic buys tissue (kidneys, corneas, skin), which keeps for days.
- **Plan "Clean Ledger" (Kessler + Derek).** The police are already looking at morgue thefts. Hand them a closed case: Vane and the Driver ran the trade (the Driver's signature is on every Harlow manifest). Vane "kills himself". The Driver "murders his brother, who found out", cleans up and runs. The Driver's body is harvested and dumped, so he's never found and stays the wanted man for good. The corpse in Derek's bed is an unclaimed body. Kessler's coroner signs it off as Derek. Derek leaves with his share.
- **Fri 18 → Sat 19 April. The last shift.** The Driver delivers the KEEP COLD box (Tomas) to Harlow and signs for it.
- **~06:00.** Derek tips the police anonymously, and the Tomas case is reopened.
- **~06:20.** Mercer shoots Vane at his desk and leaves a typed note.
- **06:38. Depot.** Mercer checks the box wasn't opened and leaves.
- **~06:45.** Derek calls the Driver home: "Grab coffee. Take your time."
- **~07:10. The flat.** The corpse in Derek's jacket is face down on the bed and Mercer is cutting it. Derek is in the bathroom with the shower running.
- **07:12–07:19.** The Driver skips the coffee, walks in early, sees "Derek" stabbed, and is killed in the living room. His phone skids under the sofa.
- **07:24.** Derek leaves the framing voicemail on that phone and goes out the back. Mercer bleaches the living room. To the police, that's the killer cleaning up.
- **Afternoon.** Harlow harvests the Driver. The rest of him goes into a ditch off the north road.
- **The Red City.** Tomas rode with the Driver all night in the box. Of everyone who handled him, only the Driver didn't know, so Tomas followed him and pulled him in.

### 1.3 Rules of the Red City
1. It holds **the dead** and **the Remnants**, the parts of living people that looked away.
2. **The dead can't refuse a direct question.** They choose only how they justify the answer.
3. **A soul is judged when it is named and says what it did.** Destroying its body changes nothing.
4. The nameless dead (the trade's "unclaimed") can't be judged. They drift as the **Lost**.
5. **The Echo** keeps everything that happened, including recordings deleted in the living world.
6. **The King never lies.** He also doesn't explain.
7. **The mark** gives light, the palm flash, Echo access, and "a shape to hold you together". Back in the living world, it's the only thing keeping the Driver's body going. **When the debt is paid, the mark lets go.**

### 2.1 New mechanic: Names
Fixes the passive hero and turns the theme into something the player does.
- **Mandatory names (3)** on the critical path: **Tomas Rourke** (office, his locker), **Ruth Ellery** (Priya's database fuse puzzle: the real name under number U-0117), and **Marcus Obi** (silo cold-room tag).
- **Optional names (up to 12):** body-bag tags in the tunnel, manifests, a few Lost in the city who whisper half a name.
- **On the bridge** each heart is armoured until you **say a name** (press F on the prompt). One named soul tears free and the heart is exposed to shoot. That's three hearts and three names.
- **Payoff:** every optional name you found appears standing with Tomas in the final bridge shot. There's no branching ending, but the shot shows what the player did. The King's line acknowledges the count.

### 2.2 How the twist is set up
Only about four clues are **visible on a first play**. The rest are replay rewards, so sharp players suspect without solving it.

**Visible on a first play**
1. The bathroom is locked with the shower running, in the real flat and again in the red flat.
2. Mercer: "Should've got the coffee."
3. The King: "Your brother is not here." / "He is not among my dead."
4. The CCTV: Derek looks into the camera and phones someone: "He thinks it was his idea."

**Replay-only**
- Derek's "Take your time."
- "Don't open anything you're not paid to open" in the red-flat memory.
- "Your brother vouched for you."
- Vane calls Kessler "Arthur".
- Kessler's tape: "keep Derek happy".
- Wall clock 07:19 vs. voicemail 07:24 (small in the phone's list, never on screen in big text).
- The anonymous tip on the TV.
- The King: "You have seen what you were shown." / "Did he."
