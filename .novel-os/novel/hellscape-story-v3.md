# HELLSCAPE — Story Revision 3.0

Polish pass on *HellScape_Story_Revamp_GDD_v2.docx* (17 Oct 2026). V2's structure, acts, gameplay and the Derek-is-alive reveal all stay. This pass fixes the plot holes, rewrites the dialogue to sound spoken, and makes the twist one that can be **solved on a replay**: every clue is in the game before the credits.

Nothing in Unity has been changed. `HellscapeDialogue.csv` is untouched (as V2 §7.1 asks).

## 2. The twist, and the clues that make it fair

**The ending twist (two hits, back to back):**
1. **Derek is alive.** He was Kessler's partner from the start, killed Tomas willingly, played "coerced" for Vane and the camera, and built the frame that makes his brother the wanted killer.

## 3. What really happened (canon)

- **13 April, 03:10.** Tomas, the night cleaner, finds bodies in a company van at Silo Two. Vane is there. Derek stabs Tomas. Vane threatens Derek with the driver ("his name's on every manifest") and believes he forced Derek into it. In fact Derek and Kessler are partners and Derek is playing scared. Vane deletes the CCTV. The office signs it off as an accident.
- **13–18 April.** Tomas's remains are held in cold storage at Silo Two. Harlow takes tissue by the gram.
- **Fri night 18th → Sat 19th.** The driver's last shift includes the refrigerated box of Tomas to Harlow. He signs for it, as he has signed every manifest that month.
- **~06:00.** Derek tips the police anonymously, so the Tomas case is reopened.
- **~06:20.** Mercer shoots Vane in his office and leaves a typed suicide note.
- **06:38.** Depot. Mercer checks the driver never opened the box, then leaves.
- **~06:45.** Derek calls the driver home and tells him to take his time.
- **~07:10.** At the flat an unclaimed body in Derek's jacket lies on the bed. Mercer is cutting it. Derek is in the bathroom with the shower running.
- **07:12–07:19.** The driver walks in early, sees "Derek" stabbed, and is killed in the living room. His phone drops on the floor, where the police will find it.
- **07:24.** Derek leaves the framing voicemail on that phone, then walks out the back.
- **Later.** The driver's body goes to Harlow, is harvested, and is dumped off the north road. The police find "Derek" in his bed and Vane's "suicide". The driver is wanted for both.

### Rules of the Zone
- The Red City holds the dead **and the part of the living that looked away** (Remnants).
- The dead can't refuse a direct question. They choose only how they justify the answer.
- A soul is judged when it **says** what it did. Destroying its body changes nothing.
- The Echo keeps everything, including recordings deleted in the living world.
- **The King never lies.** He also doesn't explain.
- The mark gives light, the flash, the Echo, and *a shape to hold you together*. Back in the living world, it's the only thing keeping the driver alive.

### Who knows what at the end
| | Knows |
|---|---|
| Driver | The organ trade, Tomas, Derek stabbing Tomas (he thinks Derek was forced), Vane, Kessler alive. Thinks Derek is dead and died believing he was guilty. |
| Vane (judged) | Everything except that Derek played him. |
| Derek and Kessler | Everything except the Red City and the driver's return. |
| Player after credits | Everything, including the heartbeat. |

## 5. V2's open decisions: recommended answers

1. **How Mercer fakes Derek's stabbing:** he doesn't stab Derek. It's an unclaimed corpse in Derek's jacket, face down. The player only ever sees jacket, hair and the knife.
2. **Maya / Mrs. Okafor:** keep Maya (she plants the signature clue). Cut Okafor for pacing.
3. **Safe code:** one combined clue. 1304 = 13 April, the night Tomas died, circled on Kessler's calendar. It opens both keypad and safe.
4. **Where harvesting happens:** at Harlow. Silo Two is cold storage and disposal, which matches "Harlow took what they could use."
5. **After the call:** black, then the heartbeat sting, then the optional title.
6. **Derek's reasons:** save them for HellScape II. The call shows *what* he did, never *why*.

## 6. QA additions (on top of V2 §7.4)

- Living-room wall clock reads before 07:24 when the driver dies. The voicemail shows 07:24.
- The bathroom stays locked, shower audible, for the whole flat sequence.
- The bedroom body's face is never visible. No line or camera shows it.
- The King has no line that is literally false.
- The Remnants are never called dead. Tomas and Vane are.
- Vane's keys are visible in Form 2 and drop in the silo before the flood.
- The CCTV phone call shows no name, caller ID or "Arthur".
- The word "Arthur" is spoken only by Vane (Act 3 and the bridge) and Derek (post-credits).

**Deliberate bends of V2 rules, for you to approve:** V2 Gate 2 said the CCTV shouldn't hint at the Derek–Kessler alliance. The "He thinks it was his idea" phone line hints at it without naming anyone. Remove those two lines if that's too much.
